# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want RegainPlus to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

Examples of positive behavior:
- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and feedback
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Assuming good intent while communicating clearly

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Encouraging harmful or unsafe use of the software

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions related to RegainPlus.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer through the repository's contact/reporting channels. Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate for the situation.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.
